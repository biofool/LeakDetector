# Executive Summary

LeakDetector helps residents report public water leaks to the responsible New Zealand council. A mobile form records the location, details, and optional photos. The public map and tracking page show progress without requiring a reporter account.

Somatic studios and related fields can use the service to report leaks near training spaces, paths, and community venues. Faster routing and duplicate detection can reduce repeated reports. Council staff receive a shared queue, service-level timers, and alerts for triage and follow-up.

The project stores optional reporter contact details and limits them to staff for the responsible council. It also removes photo metadata during image processing. Each council must still assess privacy, hosting location, retention, and operational controls before production use.
