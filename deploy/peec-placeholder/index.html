LeakDetector

NOT LIVE YET

A citizen water-leak reporting app for New Zealand councils. Residents drop a pin, take a photo, and report a leak in under 30 seconds.

  • Footpath, berm, road, meter and outside-tap leaks
  • Duplicate detection and SLA timers
  • Duty-officer email alerts and a public status map
View the project on GitHub

To report a leak today, please contact your local council directly.
